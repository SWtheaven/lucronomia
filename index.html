LucronomIA Lean

A local dashboard for monitoring content metrics and supporting editorial decisions across supported social platforms.

What the app does

LucronomIA Lean helps the LucronomIA content operation track published videos, compare platform performance, save measurement checkpoints and review content experiments.

When a platform connection is enabled, the app uses the platform's official authorization flow and APIs to request read-only metrics that the authorized account is allowed to access.

Local-first

The dashboard runs locally on the user's computer. Platform credentials and authorization tokens are handled by the local backend and are not embedded in the public website.

Read-only metrics

The integration is intended to read content and analytics data needed for reporting. It does not need permission to publish, edit or delete social media posts.
