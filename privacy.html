LUCRONOMIA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how LucronomIA Lean handles information when an authorized user connects supported social media platforms to the local dashboard.

2. Data the app may access

Depending on the platform and permissions granted by the user, LucronomIA Lean may request read-only information such as account identifiers, published-video identifiers and available performance metrics including views, likes, comments, shares, reach, saves, watch metrics and other analytics provided by the platform.

The app only requests data that the connected platform exposes to the authorized account and does not invent unavailable metrics.

3. How data is used

Platform data is used to display performance metrics, compare content results, save measurement checkpoints and support editorial decision-making for the LucronomIA content operation.

4. Storage

LucronomIA Lean is designed as a local-first application. Dashboard data is stored locally on the user's computer or browser. Authorization credentials and tokens used by the local backend are not embedded in this public website.

5. Sharing and sale of data

LucronomIA Lean does not sell platform data. The application is not designed to disclose connected-account analytics to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

6. Platform permissions

The intended integrations are read-only. LucronomIA Lean requests only the permissions needed to identify an authorized professional account, list its media and read analytics that the platform makes available. The metrics-monitoring function does not require permission to publish, edit or delete social media content.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

Users may disconnect a platform from LucronomIA Lean and may also revoke the application's authorization from the relevant platform account settings. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer request new data from that platform using the revoked authorization.

Detailed deletion instructions are available at Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's integrations or data-handling behavior changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
